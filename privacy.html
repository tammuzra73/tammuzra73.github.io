Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Aster backup is a personal backup tool used only by its owner. It uses Google Drive access limited to files it creates itself (the drive.file scope).

  • What it accesses: only the backup files it uploads to the owner's own Google Drive. It cannot see any other Drive files.
  • Where data goes: files are copied from the owner's computer directly to the owner's Google Drive. No data is sent to, stored by, or shared with anyone else.
  • Google user data: it is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used for any purpose other than this backup.
  • Removal: the owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the backup folder from Google Drive.
